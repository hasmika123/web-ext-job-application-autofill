# What your organization can see

You are joining an organization on Kiwiply. You keep your own account, and your resumes, applications and answers stay yours. This page is what you agree to share with the organization, and you choose.

## Only that I joined

The organization sees that you are a member: your name as they entered it, your email, your role, your group and when you joined. Nothing about your job search.

## Progress summary (recommended)

On top of the above, the organization sees counts and states about your job search:

- how many applications you have in each stage (saved, applied, interview, offer, rejected),
- the day you were last active,
- whether you have a resume, how strong it looks (low, mid or high) and when it last changed,
- how many interviews you have coming up (a number only),
- whether you have told us that you were placed, and when.

They never see company names, job titles, locations, salaries, your resumes or any document. They never see your saved answers, AI drafts, inbox, private notes or sign-in details.

## Everything, with one named person

Only if you choose it, and only for one named person in the organization (for example a marketer who prepares applications for you). That person can see your applications in detail, your resumes and your interview calendar, and every time they look is recorded. Your organization is never given this by default, and you are never asked to give it to "the organization".

## Your rights

- Change this at any time from Settings. Stopping takes effect immediately, on the very next request.
- Leaving the organization ends all sharing. Nothing is deleted from your account.
- We keep a short record of each change (what, when, and which text you saw) so that we can show what you agreed to.
- You must be at least 16 years old to join an organization.
- Nothing here is sold, and Kiwiply never submits an application for you.
