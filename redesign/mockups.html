From blank form to submitted in three steps

Set up your profile once. After that, applying is a click — and everything you apply to tracks itself.

1

Build your profile

Upload a resume and Kiwiply parses it into a reusable profile — contact details, work history, skills, and answers.

2

Fill any application

On a live job form, the extension scans the page and shows you exactly what it'll fill. Review, uncheck anything, then fill.

3

Watch the board fill itself

Every fill becomes a tracked application — draft on fill, confirmed on submit. No spreadsheet, no copy-paste.

Everything you need to apply faster

📄

Resume variants, parsed

AI answer drafting

Self-populating tracker
