## The model in five lines
1. A **service** is a catalog row that can be switched on and metered (key, allowance, AI budget, dependencies, audience).
2. A **plan** is a published, immutable **version** composed of services: base + included + optional add-ons with prices; for organizations a mandatory **seat** base + seat-included + seat-optional add-ons + fees (E3 reconciled: licenses are seats on an org plan version).
3. **One pricing engine** and **one margin guard** price and check every combination at every interval; annual is ON (S-01) as a derived price row whose discount admin sets under the guard.
4. **One EntitlementService** resolves what a person or seat holds, with a `LOCKED` state for read-only fallback; no cross-world stacking (O4).
5. **Stripe stays the truth**; there are no existing subscribers today (S-03) so the seed is a no-data migration with the safety rules kept for future cohorts; retirements default to Free with read-only locks (S-19/S-20), never cut a paid period short and never delete data.

## Worked numbers (from `03 §8`, USD minor units, simple fee model)
- **Individual, monthly:** Pro 1999 + Extra AI credits ×2 (499 each) = **2997/mo**; AI window 450 ¢; every row and the composition pass the 80 % floor (8059 / 8216 / 8108 bp).
- **Individual, yearly (if enabled, 10 % off):** 21589 + 2 × 5389 = **32367/yr** ≈ 2697/mo, saves 3597; a 15 % discount is refused by the guard (7929 bp) — max 11.5 % on Pro at Launch-1 prices.
- **Organization, monthly:** setup fee 49900 once; seats A 2797 + B 3696 + C 1499 = **7992/mo** (golden G1).
- **Organization, yearly (if enabled, 10 % plan discount, 9 % on AI Job Matching, 6 % on Tailoring — the guard's levers):** **86756/yr** ≈ 7230/mo, saves 9148; adding a seat on day 100 costs 17270 prorated; a seat transfer costs 0.

## Runbooks (filled in by the stories that own them)
- **No-data migration** (K8.4, S-03: there are no existing subscribers): the Liquibase changeSets seed the services and plans (no prices, B-01) and run `SubscriptionBackfill` (zero rows today; idempotent; a Pro row matching no configured price id is linked to PRO v1 without an interval and logged, `SUBSCRIPTION_BACKFILL_STRICT=true` fails instead). Then `BackfillPeriodStartJob` (hourly, also callable with a cursor) stamps `current_period_start` from Stripe invoices: read-only (`listInvoices` only), 50 rows a run, resumable (a Stripe outage stops at the failing row, the next run continues there), skipped with one log line when the Stripe key is blank. Verify: `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM subscription WHERE plan_version_id IS NULL` is 0 after the linker ran, and `SELECT COUNT(*) FROM subscription WHERE stripe_subscription_id IS NOT NULL AND current_period_start IS NULL` drains to 0. Rollback: set `billing.catalog.shadow=true` (the legacy gates answer again); the added columns and tables stay.
- **Test clocks** (K8.6): scenario P (personal: checkout with add-ons monthly and yearly, change, failed renewal → lapse, resubscribe) and scenario R (retirement: notice → choice → Free fallback with locks), test mode only, object ids recorded here.
- **Annual operations** (K9.6; decision S-01 annual is ON, S-09 tax). Turning yearly billing on, and keeping it on, is a short sequence with checks:
  1. **Tax mode decided (S-09), before the first yearly invoice.** A yearly invoice is twelve times larger, so a tax surprise is twelve times larger. Record the decision in Admin > Settings, `billing.tax.mode`: `STRIPE_TAX` (Stripe Tax calculates it; also turn on the Stripe-side automatic tax, `STRIPE_AUTOMATIC_TAX`) or `NOT_COLLECTING` (a recorded decision not to collect yet). The default `UNDECIDED` makes the billing self-check (`BillingSelfCheck`) log `billing self-check ANNUAL_TAX_UNDECIDED` at every start while `billing.annual.enabled` is true. The check warns; it does not refuse, because annual shipped ON by decision S-01. Treat the warning as a release-checklist line: no yearly invoice in production until it is gone.
  2. **Fee model chosen (S-11).** `billing.fees.model` is `SIMPLE` (the model the locked prices were set with) unless you switch it; look at Admin > Pricing > margin before publishing a yearly discount.
  3. **Derived YEAR rows published with the plans.** Publishing a plan version derives the YEAR price from the MONTH price under `billing.annual.default_discount_bp` (component and plan overrides win) and runs the margin guard on every row (at the locked prices the simple model allows at most about 11.5 % on Pro). A failing yearly row blocks the publish; it is never overridable. Verify on `/pricing` that the Yearly tab shows "billed once a year", the monthly equivalent and the saving.
  4. **Switching rules to expect** (K5.6/K9.2, all in `SwitchRules`): an add-on bought mid-term is prorated on the annual price over the remaining seconds of the term (23787 on day 100 of 365 costs 17270); removals, downgrades and yearly-to-monthly take effect at the term end and nothing is credited or refunded; monthly-to-yearly applies at the next monthly renewal (no partial-period mixing); an upgrade now charges per `billing.upgrade.proration`.
  5. **Monitoring.** `entitlement.shadow_mismatch` stays at zero; the personal reconciler (K8.5) shows no drift for a week; the realised margin report rows per interval (B2.4) are a follow-up of the margin report, not built yet.
  6. **Switch-off rollback.** Set `billing.annual.enabled=false` in Admin > Settings with a reason. New YEAR prices are refused and the Yearly tab disappears; every subscription already on a yearly term keeps running to the end of its term (the quote policy still prices it), and a yearly subscriber can still leave to monthly at term end. Nothing is refunded. Switch it back on to resume selling yearly.
- **Cut-over: the launch Pro prices without retyping them** (decision B-01, answered 2026-10-16). The catalog ships without prices, so PRO v1 is a DRAFT until prices exist. On a box that already sells Pro through `STRIPE_PRICE_MONTHLY` and `STRIPE_PRICE_3MO`, an administrator runs, once: `POST /api/admin/plans/PRO/versions/1/import-stripe-prices` with `{"reason":"Cut-over: import the launch prices"}`. It reads those two price ids from Stripe (Stripe is the truth) and creates the matching DRAFT `product_price` rows (MONTH and QUARTER, amount and currency from Stripe, `stripe_price_id` set). It is idempotent, never overwrites a row, needs billing on (503 `BILLING_DISABLED` otherwise), is `ROLE_ADMIN` only and audited as HIGH (`PLAN_PRICES_IMPORTED`). Then publish PRO v1 from the plan builder: the margin guard still runs, the published rows keep their Stripe price ids (no new Stripe Price is created), and existing subscribers keep paying the same price. Once PRO v1 is published the old settings are only a fallback: delete `STRIPE_PRICE_MONTHLY`, `STRIPE_PRICE_3MO`, `DOSSIER_AI_PRO_MONTHLY_BUDGET_USD` and `DOSSIER_AI_PRO_3MO_BUDGET_USD` after one release (K1.6: checkout, the AI budget, the resume caps and the admin MRR read the catalog when the setting is blank or the plan is published).
- **Publishing guards** (decisions W-4, P-15, W-6, P-02, answered 2026-10-16). A plan or price publish is refused: 503 `BILLING_DISABLED` when Stripe is off (blank key), 422 `LIVE_KEY_NOT_ALLOWED` when a LIVE Stripe key is configured outside the `prod` profile, 422 `SETUP_FEE_REQUIRED` for an organization plan without a setup fee. A QA or local stack without Stripe turns on Admin > Settings `billing.publish.allow_without_stripe` (HIGH, audited, default false; also settable by the deployment property `dossier.billing.publish-allow-without-stripe`): the publish then records the version and creates no Stripe Prices; they follow at the next publish that needs them. That setting never lifts the live-key refusal.
- **Shadow flip** (K7.7), `billing.catalog.shadow` true to false. The legacy gates answer while it is true and the resolver only runs beside them. Checklist, in order: (1) `ShadowCompareTest`, `PlanResolverTest`, `EntitlementServiceTest`, `EntitlementServiceShadowTest`, `LegacyReadersUnchangedIT`, `BillingResourceIT`, `BillingWebhookIT` green on the release candidate; (2) the log search `entitlement.shadow_mismatch` (and the Prometheus counter of the same name) shows zero for one release (no two-week wait, there is no existing cohort, S-03); (3) `PRO` and `AUTOPILOT` v1 are linked (`plan_version_id` not null on every paid row) and the prices an admin published pass the margin guard; (4) flip in Admin > Settings with a reason; (5) watch `entitlement.shadow_mismatch` and 402 rates for an hour; (6) rollback = set it back to true (instant, nothing else changes). Until the flip the only user-visible resolver output is the additive `features[]`, `limits`, `aiPct` and `periodEnd` on `GET /api/profile/version`.

## Conventions
Design docs say what exists with file references and what to build with table/column/endpoint names. Money
is `long` minor units + ISO currency; percentages are basis points; the only rounding is
`Minor.mulDivHalfEven`. Every price, cap, interval, discount and floor is a row or a settings key. The web
never computes money. Anything undecided goes to `decisions-for-user.md`.
