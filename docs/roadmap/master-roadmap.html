Legend and how progress is counted

Each story is worth points by size (S = 1, M = 2, L = 3). A story contributes points times its status credit. Epic, area and overall percentages are earned points divided by total points.

StatusCredit
Done100%
In review75%
Partial50%
In progress25%
Not started0%
Blocked0%

P0 must ship for launch, P1 next, P2 later. P0 progress counts only stories in P0 epics. ★ marks a critical story. Gen Z marks an epic whose stories explain why younger users will love it. A dashed chip is a dependency that is not in any roadmap file.

"Next up" lists the first not-started stories in P0 epics whose dependencies are all done.

Shortcuts: / focuses search. Links like #/user/U2/U2.3 open a story directly.
